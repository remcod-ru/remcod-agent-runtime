# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, precise, and considerate. Give and accept technical feedback in good faith, acknowledge uncertainty, respect privacy, and focus disagreement on ideas and evidence.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publication of another person's private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communication that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently exclude participants for repeated or severe violations. Enforcement reports must use the same private reporting path described in `SECURITY.md`; do not expose sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers will protect the privacy and safety of reporters to the extent practical and will disclose conflicts of interest. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and open to review by an uninvolved maintainer when available.
